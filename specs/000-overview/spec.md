- **Status:** Approved

## 1. Context and goal

SuperCool Finances needs a balance service for its customers' money: customer accounts, a double-entry ledger, deposits, withdrawals, transfers and reversals. Customer money must never be **lost** (every movement is atomic and balanced), **duplicated** (a retried request applies at most once) or **exposed** (a customer can see and move only their own money).

This spec holds what every other spec relies on: the actors and their roles, the glossary, the supported currencies, the global invariants and the non-functional requirements. Each capability spec defines its own behaviour and errors and uses the terms below with exactly these meanings. Error status codes follow one rule for every spec:

- **400**: the request is broken. It cannot be parsed, lacks a required header or carries a malformed one, or carries an invalid pagination cursor.
- **409**: the request conflicts with the current state of the resource it acts on, for example closing an account that holds money, changing the status of a closed account, reversing a transaction already reversed, or reusing an idempotency key whose first request is still in progress.
- **422**: the request was understood and refused. Either its content fails validation, its `Idempotency-Key` was already used by the same user for a different request (spec 005), or it is a money movement refused by a business rule, including a movement on a frozen or closed account.

### 1.1 Actors and roles

| Actor        | Who                                                                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Customer** | An authenticated user who owns customer accounts.                                                |
| **Operator** | An authenticated staff user. Simulates the payment rails and runs back-office actions.           |
| **System**   | The service itself. Owns the system accounts that settle money entering and leaving the service. |

Each authenticated user has exactly one role, `customer` or `operator`. A person who needs both roles uses two identities, so every action is attributed to one role. Operations by role:

| Operation                                      | Customer                                                                  | Operator                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Read an account: details and balance           | Own accounts only                                                         | Any customer account                              |
| List accounts                                  | Own accounts only                                                         | No (ACC-R27)                                      |
| List an account's history (its ledger entries) | Own accounts only                                                         | Any customer account                              |
| Read a transaction                             | Transactions with an entry on own accounts, only those entries (spec 003) | Any transaction, every entry                      |
| Deposit (simulates money arriving from a rail) | No                                                                        | Any customer account, never a system account      |
| Withdraw                                       | From own accounts only                                                    | No                                                |
| Transfer                                       | Out of own accounts                                                       | No                                                |
| Reverse a transaction                          | No                                                                        | Any transaction that is not a reversal (spec 004) |
| Freeze, unfreeze or close an account           | No                                                                        | Any customer account, never a system account      |
| Open an account                                | Own accounts                                                              | No (ACC-R06)                                      |

No caller acts as the system: system accounts are moved only as the other side of a customer movement. A caller whose role is not permitted an operation gets 403 (SYS-R04): which operations exist is public, so a 403 reveals nothing, while 404 stays for resources the caller may not see.

### 1.2 Glossary

| Term                   | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account**            | A container of money in a single currency, fixed when it is opened. A customer who needs several currencies opens one account per currency. Either a customer account or a system account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Customer account**   | An account owned by exactly one customer. It has a status (active, frozen or closed) and a cached balance. The effect of each status on movements is defined in the accounts spec.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **System account**     | An internal account owned by the service. In this version the only system accounts are the settlement accounts. It has no owner, no cached balance and may go below zero. No movement updates its row or takes `FOR UPDATE`, `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` or `FOR SHARE` on it; the `FOR KEY SHARE` taken by a foreign key check is accepted (section 1.3 of spec 002).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Settlement account** | The system account of one currency that is the other side of every deposit and withdrawal in that currency, representing the external payment rails. Exactly one exists per currency of table 1.3, with code `external-settlement:<currency>`, created by a migration and never through the API (LED-R08). A reversal mirrors its original's accounts, settlement account included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Currency**           | An ISO 4217 alphabetic code from the table in 1.3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Minor units**        | The smallest unit of a currency. A currency's exponent is the number of minor units in one major unit as a power of ten: exponent 2 means 100 cents per dollar, exponent 0 means the major unit is the minor unit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Amount**             | An integer number of minor units together with a currency. `bigint` in the domain; in the API a string of decimal digits plus a currency code, for example `{"amount": "1050", "currency": "EUR"}` for 10.50 EUR and `{"amount": "1050", "currency": "JPY"}` for 1050 yen. Never a float or JSON number. Every amount fits 1 to 9223372036854775807 minor units, the PostgreSQL `bigint` maximum; zero and negative amounts are refused (SYS-R07), and the direction of a movement comes from its kind, never from the sign of its amount. The ledger spec adds a configurable maximum per movement, `MAX_AMOUNT_MINOR`, default "100000000000" minor units (LED-R23). |
| **Money movement**     | A deposit, withdrawal, transfer or reversal. Each one is recorded as exactly one transaction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Transaction**        | The atomic, immutable record of one money movement: two or more ledger entries whose amounts sum to zero per currency.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Ledger entry**       | One line of a transaction: an account and a signed amount in that account's currency. A positive amount adds to the account's balance, a negative one subtracts from it, so a transaction balances when its amounts sum to zero. Entries are append-only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Ledger**             | All transactions and their entries. The source of truth for where money went.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Balance**            | The sum of an account's ledger entries. For a customer account it is also cached on the account and updated in the same database transaction as its entries. For a system account it is always derived from its entries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Idempotency key**    | A client-chosen value sent in the `Idempotency-Key` header of every money-moving POST, and optionally of account creation (spec 001). It is scoped to the user and bound to a fingerprint of method, path and canonical body, so a retry returns the original result instead of moving money again. Defined in the idempotency spec.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Idempotency record** | The row that stores, per user and `Idempotency-Key`, the request's fingerprint and the response it got. Spec 005 calls it the key row.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Idempotent replay**  | The answer to a request whose key and fingerprint match a stored idempotency record: the stored status and body, with the header `Idempotent-Replayed: true` (SYS-R33).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Reversal**           | An operator action that corrects a transaction by recording a new, compensating transaction whose entries mirror the original's. The original is never changed. Defined in spec 004.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Problem details**    | The body of every error response: `application/problem+json` as defined by RFC 9457, with a `type` of the form `/problems/<slug>`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Correlation id**     | An identifier for one request, returned in the response, written in every log line of the request and stored with its audit record. It travels in the `X-Request-Id` header (SYS-R21); a client's value is kept only when it is made of the characters SYS-R21 allows, so it cannot inject into logs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Audit record**       | An append-only record of who did which money movement or account status change, when and in which request: a row of an append-only audit table, written in the database transaction of the change it records. Only committed changes get one; attempts that fail appear only in the logs. Status changes are audited by spec 001 (ACC-R26). Its `action` is the kind of movement (`deposit`, `withdrawal`, `transfer`, `reversal`) or the status change.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

### 1.3 Supported currencies

The minor-unit exponent of every currency comes from this table (AGENTS.md). USD, MXN, EUR and COP are the currencies of the assumed launch markets. JPY is included because it has no minor units, so that case stays covered by tests.

| Code | Currency       | Exponent | Minor unit   |
| ---- | -------------- | -------- | ------------ |
| USD  | US dollar      | 2        | cent         |
| MXN  | Mexican peso   | 2        | centavo      |
| EUR  | Euro           | 2        | cent         |
| COP  | Colombian peso | 2        | centavo      |
| JPY  | Japanese yen   | 0        | none (1 yen) |

## 3. Acceptance criteria

Unless stated otherwise: customer user C1 owns account A1, customer user C2 owns account B1, operator user O1 is an operator, balances are set up by deposits from O1, and every deposit, withdrawal, transfer and reversal carries a fresh Idempotency-Key unless the AC names one or says it has none.

### SYS-AC01 · Each operation is permitted only to its role

- **Level:** integration
- **Covers:** SYS-R03, SYS-R04, SYS-R31
- **Given** C1 owns A1 with balance "10000" EUR, C2 owns B1 with "0" EUR and B2 with "0" EUR, O1 is an operator, U is an account id that does not exist, S is the EUR settlement account, and T is a transaction id that does not exist
- **When** each decided row of table 1.1 is requested once by C1 and once by O1, with a fresh Idempotency-Key for every POST: a read of A1, a deposit of "100" EUR into A1, a withdrawal of "100" EUR from A1, a transfer of "100" EUR from A1 to B1, a read of that transfer, a reversal of that deposit, and a freeze, unfreeze and close of B2
- **Then** every request the table permits succeeds; every request it does not permit (for example a deposit by C1, or a withdrawal from A1 by O1) answers 403 with type `/problems/forbidden`, and the ledger, balances and account statuses are unchanged by it; and when C1 also deposits into, freezes, unfreezes and closes U and S, and reverses T, every one of those requests answers 403 with a body equal, except for `requestId`, to C1's 403 for the deposit into A1

### SYS-AC02 · Requests without valid credentials are rejected

- **Level:** integration
- **Covers:** SYS-R01, SYS-R02, SYS-R30, SYS-R32, SYS-R36
- **Given** A1 owned by C1 with balance "10000" EUR
- **When** a read of A1 and a withdrawal of "100" EUR from A1 are sent with no credentials, with an expired token, with a token whose signature does not verify, with a token whose header says `alg: none`, with a token signed with HS256 but without a `role` claim, with one whose `role` is "admin", with one without `exp`, with one without `sub`, and with tokens signed with HS512 and with RS256; and `GET /no-such-path` is sent without credentials and with C1's valid token

## 5. Invariants

These hold before and after every operation of every capability. Each one is proven by the acceptance criteria listed with it.

- The entries of every transaction sum to zero per currency (SYS-AC08; LED-AC02, LED-AC04, LED-AC05, REV-AC03).
- No customer account balance is ever below zero; system account balances may be (LED-AC07, LED-AC08, MOV-AC11, MOV-AC13, REV-AC10, REV-AC22).
- The sum of all balances per currency, system accounts included, is zero (SYS-AC11; LED-AC10, LED-AC15, LED-AC17).
- The cached balance of every customer account equals the sum of its ledger entries (SYS-AC12; LED-AC07, LED-AC15, LED-AC17, MOV-AC13, MOV-AC14).
- Transactions and ledger entries are never updated or deleted (LED-AC11, LED-AC12, REV-AC01).
- System accounts have no cached balance, and no movement updates or locks their row (SYS-AC12; LED-AC06, LED-AC09, MOV-AC15, REV-AC20).
- A request with an `Idempotency-Key` is applied at most once per user and key, on any replica (SYS-AC14; IDM-AC07, IDM-AC10, IDM-AC21, DEP-AC11).
- A customer never reads or moves money in an account they do not own, and cannot tell another customer's account from one that does not exist (SYS-AC03; ACC-AC09, AUT-AC09, AUT-AC16, MOV-AC10).
- Every applied money movement has exactly one audit record, and a rejected one has none (MOV-AC16, REV-AC18, IDM-AC07).

## 6. Out of scope

- Exchange between currencies (FX): a movement never converts money, and accounts in different currencies never exchange it.
- Interest and fees.
- Statements and reports for customers beyond reading an account and its history.
- KYC and AML checks.
- Multi-region deployment and cross-region replication.
- Real payment rails: deposits by an operator simulate money arriving, and withdrawals record money leaving through the settlement account.
